THE AUTHOR

Meet Bradley Hobbs

Christian Author • Biblical Teacher • Lifelong Student of Scripture

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Writing to Point Readers Back to Scripture

Bradley Hobbs is a Christian author and biblical teacher whose writing explores Scripture, spiritual growth, leadership, obedience, accountability, and the condition of the heart. Through books, devotional writing, and teaching resources, he seeks to encourage readers to examine their lives honestly and grow in their walk with Jesus Christ.

His work is shaped by the conviction that biblical truth should be brought into the realities of everyday life. Questions about faith, relationships, church life, and spiritual leadership deserve careful attention, humility, and a willingness to let the Word of God challenge our assumptions.

THE HEART BEHIND THE WRITING

Truth, Humility, and a Teachable Heart

Bradley writes for readers who want to understand Scripture more deeply and apply it faithfully. His approach encourages personal reflection rather than simply evaluating the actions of others. Each reader is invited to ask what the Word of God reveals, where growth is needed, and how faith can be expressed through choices, character, and conduct.

He believes spiritual maturity requires more than position, knowledge, or outward appearance. It calls for humility, a willingness to receive correction, discernment, and a heart that remains accountable to God. These themes are especially important when discussing authority and leadership, where biblical responsibility must never be separated from the character and truth of God.

“The purpose is not to place a human voice above Scripture, but to encourage every reader to return to the Word of God with an open heart and a willingness to obey truth.”

ABOUT THIS BOOK

Why Beyond the Veil Was Written

Beyond the Veil: Revealing the Truth About Authority, Obedience, and the Heart examines the relationship between God's authority and human leadership. Across its chapters, the book considers submission, the cost of obedience, the responsibilities of spiritual leaders, the misuse of authority, and the importance of allowing God to correct the heart.

The aim is to direct readers toward biblical discernment and personal responsibility. Authority should be examined through Scripture, leadership should be exercised with humility, and every believer should remember that all people ultimately answer to God.

Bradley hopes the book will support thoughtful personal study, meaningful church discussion, and a deeper commitment to living in the light of biblical truth.

A Continuing Invitation

Thank you for taking time to learn more about the author and the heart behind this book. May your reading lead you beyond the printed page into prayer, honest reflection, careful study of Scripture, and a more faithful walk with Jesus Christ.
