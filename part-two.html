By Bradley Hobbs

What Lies Beneath the Surface

There are moments when our true understanding of God's authority is revealed not through what we profess, but through how we respond when obedience becomes difficult. It is easy to speak about submission when we agree with the instruction, trust the person delivering it, and understand the outcome we expect. The deeper examination begins when our expectations are challenged, our judgment is questioned, or circumstances require us to choose between protecting our preferences and remaining faithful to the truth of God's Word.

In this part of our journey, we will examine what happens within the heart when obedience carries a cost. We will consider how believers respond to correction, disappointment, disagreement, and the decisions of those entrusted with authority. We will also explore the importance of discernment, recognizing that spiritual maturity requires both a willingness to receive instruction and the wisdom to distinguish biblical authority from human demands that exceed their rightful limits.

Our responses during difficult moments can reveal attitudes that remain hidden when life is comfortable. Pride may become visible when we are corrected, resentment may surface when we experience disappointment, and fear may influence our decisions when we believe that speaking honestly could cost us a relationship or position. These moments are opportunities to examine ourselves before God and allow His Word to shape our responses.

The purpose is not to encourage suspicion toward those who lead, nor is it to excuse resistance whenever an instruction is uncomfortable. Rather, we must learn to respond with humility, discernment, and integrity while recognizing that both believers and leaders remain accountable to God. Obedience must be rooted in truth, and authority must be exercised according to the principles of Scripture.

As we look beneath the surface, we will discover that the question is not simply whether we follow instructions, but what governs our hearts when following God requires something from us. The answer will influence how we respond to leadership, how we handle disappointment, and how faithfully we walk with Jesus Christ when the path before us is not the one we would have chosen.

COMPANION CLASSROOM

Part Two Study Room

This part of Beyond the Veil includes a companion learning path for readers, church classes, and small groups. Read each chapter first, then use its study material to move from understanding the teaching to personally applying it. The study room is designed for individual reflection or guided group discussion.

Focus for this part: The cost of obedience, responses to correction, discernment, and the difference between faithful submission and demands that conflict with Scripture.

What each chapter’s study area includes

  • Personal Reflection and Application: prompts to examine your own attitudes, choices, relationships, and next steps.
  • Study Questions: questions for individual study, classroom discussion, or small groups, grounded in the chapter’s teaching and Scripture references.
  • Closing Prayer: a guided response to the chapter’s message and an opportunity to bring your commitments before Jesus Christ.
  • Affirmation of Faith: a first person declaration that reinforces the chapter’s central truth.
  • Looking Ahead: a short transition and link to the next chapter.

These materials are intended to support learning and conversation, not replace reading the chapter or careful study of Scripture. Participants should be encouraged to respond honestly, listen respectfully, and remember that personal reflections are their own.
