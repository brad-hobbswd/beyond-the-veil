COMPANION CLASSROOM · PART ONE: DISCOVERING GOD’S AUTHORITY

Chapter 1 Study Room

The Authority Behind Everything

Read · Reflect · Discuss · Apply

Use this companion page after reading Chapter 1. It is designed for personal reflection, church classes, and small groups. Take time to consider each question honestly. In a group setting, allow participants to share only what they feel comfortable sharing, listen respectfully, and keep the discussion grounded in Scripture.

Foundational Scripture References

Psalm 115:3; Isaiah 46:9 to 10; Matthew 28:18; John 14:15; Acts 5:29; Romans 13:1 to 7; Hebrews 4:12 to 13

Read the references in context and consider how they relate to the chapter's central teaching.

Personal Reflection and Application

How does recognizing God as the highest authority change the way you respond to correction, leadership, and difficult decisions?

Where do you need to become more teachable or more faithful in applying Scripture?

Study Questions

  1. What is the difference between God's supreme authority and the responsibility entrusted to human beings?
  2. How can you honor legitimate leadership without placing any person above God's Word?
  3. How does recognizing God as the highest authority change the way you respond to correction, leadership, and difficult decisions?
  4. Where do you need to become more teachable or more faithful in applying Scripture?

For group discussion: Invite participants to connect their answers to the Scripture references and identify one practical lesson they can carry into daily life.

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, help me receive the truth of this chapter with humility and an open heart. Search my motives, correct my understanding where it needs to change, and give me courage to apply Your Word faithfully. Teach me to act with wisdom, integrity, and love, whether I am receiving guidance or carrying responsibility for others. Protect me from pride, fear, resentment, and the desire to control what belongs in Your hands. Help me pursue truth without bitterness, receive correction without defensiveness, and take practical steps toward greater faithfulness. Let my life reflect Your character and remain accountable to Your Word. In the name of Jesus Christ, amen.

Affirmation of Faith

I will seek to live according to the truth of Jesus Christ. I will examine my own heart, receive correction with humility, and exercise discernment with wisdom. I will take responsibility for my choices, treat others with dignity, and remember that every human responsibility remains accountable to God. I will put the truth I have learned into practice and continue growing in faithfulness, integrity, and love.

Looking Ahead

Carry the central lesson of this chapter into your daily decisions. Continue to the next chapter and allow each part of the book to build upon the foundation of truth, humility, and accountability.
