CHAPTER STUDY GUIDE · PART ONE: DISCOVERING GOD’S AUTHORITY

Chapter 2 Study Guide

The Heart of Submission

Read · Reflect · Discuss · Apply

Use this companion page after reading Chapter 2. It is designed for personal reflection, church classes, and small groups. Take time to consider each question honestly. In a group setting, allow participants to share only what they feel comfortable sharing, listen respectfully, and keep the discussion grounded in Scripture.

Foundational Scripture References

John 14:15; Romans 12:1 to 2; Ephesians 5:21; James 4:7; 1 Peter 5:5 to 6

Read the references in context and consider how they relate to the chapter's central teaching.

Personal Reflection and Application

Take time to consider how you respond when your preferences conflict with what you understand Scripture to require. Are you willing to reconsider your position when new understanding reveals that you may be mistaken, or do you tend to defend yourself before carefully examining the truth? Consider whether you have confused genuine submission with outward compliance, or whether past experiences have made it difficult for you to trust anyone in a position of responsibility.

If you are a leader, examine whether you welcome sincere questions and remain willing to acknowledge mistakes. Consider whether the people entrusted to you can raise legitimate concerns without being treated as disloyal. If you have experienced the misuse of authority, allow yourself to acknowledge the harm without assuming that every form of leadership is inherently harmful. Seek wisdom from Scripture and trustworthy sources of support as you determine how to respond faithfully.

Choose one practical area in which you can demonstrate a more surrendered heart this week. It may involve receiving correction without becoming defensive, acknowledging a mistake, seeking clarity before making an assumption, or addressing a concern honestly and respectfully. Do not approach this exercise as an attempt to prove your spiritual maturity. Approach it as an opportunity to grow in your relationship with God and allow His truth to shape your conduct.

Study Questions

  1. Why must submission begin with our relationship with God rather than with our response to human leaders?
  2. What is the difference between outward compliance and genuine surrender of the heart?
  3. How does Proverbs 3:5 to 6 challenge our tendency to rely exclusively upon our own understanding?
  4. What can we learn from the example of Jesus in Matthew 26:39 about obedience when doing God’s will involves suffering or sacrifice?
  5. Why is it important to distinguish submission to God from unquestioning compliance with human authority?
  6. How can pride influence the way believers and leaders respond to correction?
  7. What responsibilities do spiritual leaders have when exercising authority, and why is accountability essential to faithful leadership?
  8. What practical changes can help you cultivate a more teachable and surrendered heart?

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus Christ, I come before You asking that You would examine my heart and reveal the places where I have struggled to surrender my desires, preferences, and expectations to Your truth. Forgive me for the times I have defended my own understanding without first seeking Your wisdom, and help me recognize when pride, fear, or resentment has influenced my decisions. Give me a heart that remains teachable and a spirit that is willing to receive correction when I need to change.

Teach me to understand submission according to Your Word rather than through fear, manipulation, or the expectations of other people. Help me honor those who faithfully serve while maintaining the discernment necessary to recognize the limits of human authority. When I face difficult circumstances, give me the wisdom to distinguish between a personal preference and a biblical conviction, and help me respond with humility when I must reconsider my position.

If I have been wounded by the misuse of authority, bring healing to the places where disappointment has made it difficult for me to trust. Help me pursue truth without becoming consumed by bitterness, and give me courage to establish appropriate boundaries when they are necessary. Teach me to forgive without pretending that wrongdoing does not matter, and help me seek accountability with a sincere desire for righteousness rather than revenge.

For every responsibility You have entrusted to me, give me the humility to serve faithfully. If I lead others, keep me from allowing pride or insecurity to influence my decisions. Make me willing to acknowledge mistakes, receive correction, and treat others with the dignity and compassion that reflect Your character. Let my conduct demonstrate the same principles I encourage others to follow.

Above all, create in me a clean heart and renew a steadfast spirit within me. Help me to love Your truth, trust Your wisdom, and follow You with sincerity even when obedience is difficult. May my life reflect a genuine surrender to Your lordship, and may every decision I make become an opportunity to honor You. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

Affirmation of Faith

My heart belongs to Jesus Christ, and I choose to submit my life to His truth. I will seek God’s wisdom rather than rely exclusively upon my own understanding, and I will remain willing to receive correction when I need to grow. I will honor godly leadership without placing any human being above the authority of God. I will exercise discernment without allowing pride to control my decisions, and I will pursue truth without surrendering my heart to bitterness. If I am entrusted with leadership, I will remember that authority carries responsibility and that my conduct must remain accountable to God. I choose humility over pride, obedience over self will, and spiritual maturity over the need to prove myself right. By the grace of God, I will continue learning to live with a heart that is surrendered to Jesus Christ.

Looking Ahead

A surrendered heart must eventually be tested through the decisions we make and the circumstances we face. It is one thing to speak about obedience when the path is comfortable, but another to remain faithful when doing what is right requires sacrifice, patience, or the willingness to release something we deeply desire. In the next chapter, we will examine When Obedience Costs You and consider how believers can remain faithful to God when obedience challenges their expectations, tests their character, and requires them to trust Him beyond what they can presently understand.
