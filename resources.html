Resources for Reflection and Support

Spiritual growth includes prayer, careful study of Scripture, wise counsel, and the courage to seek appropriate support when a situation requires more than personal reflection.

Personal Bible study

Consider studying these passages in context and writing down what they reveal about God's character, the condition of the heart, humility, correction, leadership, and personal responsibility.

  • Matthew 20:25–28
  • Acts 5:29
  • Romans 12:2
  • Galatians 6:1–5
  • 1 Thessalonians 5:21
  • 2 Timothy 2:24–25
  • Hebrews 13:7, 17
  • 1 Peter 5:2–3

When you need additional support

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States, call or text 988 for crisis support. For domestic violence support, contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 800-799-7233 or visit thehotline.org.

Seeking help from a qualified counselor, advocate, or other trusted professional can be appropriate when dealing with trauma, coercion, abuse, or serious emotional distress. Seeking support does not mean you have failed spiritually.

These resources are starting points, not endorsements of every statement or service offered by an external organization.
