ABOUT THE BOOK

More than outward obedience

Beyond the Veil is a Christian teaching book inviting readers to examine what Scripture reveals about authority, submission, obedience, leadership, correction, and personal responsibility before God.

True spiritual maturity is not measured by silence, appearances, or unquestioning loyalty to a person. It requires a heart that remains surrendered to Jesus Christ, a willingness to receive correction, and the discernment to recognize that every human leader is accountable to God.

This book calls readers to pursue truth without bitterness, establish healthy spiritual boundaries, and allow God to work first in their own hearts.

A PERSONAL INVITATION

Let truth begin in the heart

The purpose of this study is not to encourage accusation or division. It is to help each reader seek God honestly, recognize the responsibility that comes with influence, and respond to His Word with humility and courage.
